01 / OUR VISION

A LIVING CULTURAL ORGANISM.

Spiral Cactus brings independent music, visual expression and creative communities into one evolving ecosystem.

Our vision is to bridge the gap between small DIY spaces and large institutions: preserving the freedom of underground culture while creating the conditions for ambitious, professional and inclusive experiences.

The brand is already taking shape through events, collaborations and the people who gather around it. The longer-term ambition is a hybrid venue and cultural platform in Brussels.

04 / THE LONG-TERM AMBITION

A NEW KIND OF CULTURAL HOME.

VENUE PROJECT — IN DEVELOPMENT

Our goal is to develop a hybrid venue of approximately 500 capacity in Brussels, combining an immersive club, an art laboratory and a platform for collectives and cultural partners.

A place intended to live beyond the night — with workshops, exhibitions, collaborative formats and creative transmission. These are planned developments, not services currently available in a permanent Spiral Cactus venue.
