01 /WHO WE ARE TODAY
BRUSSELS ROOTS. UNDERGROUND SOUNDS.

BEYOND NIGHTLIFE

SpiralCactus is an independent Brussels collective creating underground rave nights, collaborating with artists and bringing people together through music. Our home today is the community we build at each event.

01

A club

A platform for alternative music, independent events and immersive creative performances.

02

A lab

A space to experiment with DJing, production, sound, light, exhibitions and collaborations.

03

A community

People, artists, independent crews and associations building a culture together.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Underground means freedom, not exclusion. We believe in adventurous music, independent artists, quality experiences and a dancefloor where respect comes first.

01 /

ALL IDENTITIES WELCOME

There is no place for racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia or discrimination. Everybody deserves respect.

02 /

CONSENT & BOUNDARIES

Respect personal space and boundaries. No unwanted touching, pressure, harassment or intimidation.

03 /

LOOK OUT FOR EACH OTHER

Pay attention to the people around you. If something feels wrong, speak to the venue staff or a SpiralCactus organiser.

04 /

RESPECT THE SPACE

Respect artists, staff, the crowd and our neighbours. Ask before photographing others; help keep the experience welcoming.

Freedom on the dancefloor comes with responsibility to one another.

02 /OUR VISION FOR TOMORROW
THE NEXT CHAPTER

A PERMANENT HOME

Today our events take place in existing Brussels venues. Our long-term ambition is to establish a permanent SpiralCactus space for around 500 people, with professional sound, immersive visuals and room for club nights, creative learning, exhibitions and community projects. This is a future project, not an existing venue.

WHERE WE WANT TO GO

A living cultural home

We are not trying to become just another party promoter. SpiralCactus aims to connect underground scenes, offer emerging artists real opportunities, bring together sound, light and visual storytelling, and create a lasting cultural meeting point in Brussels. Our long-term ambition is to open a permanent physical venue where club nights coexist with daytime workshops, exhibitions, artist residencies, community projects and collaborations—an inclusive space that can grow with the city. This venue is a future project, not an existing location.
