THE SPIRALCACTUS PROJECT

BEYOND NIGHTLIFE

SpiralCactus is an emerging cultural collective rooted in Brussels, joining independent music, art and community through shared experiences.

01

A club

A platform for alternative music, independent events and immersive creative performances.

02

A lab

A space to experiment with DJing, production, sound, light, exhibitions and collaborations.

03

A community

People, artists, independent crews and associations building a culture together.

Our philosophy

Underground — Organic — Brussels. We stand for artistic independence, open-minded programming, high production standards, respect, inclusivity and a nightlife where every scene can feel represented.

A permanent home: our next chapter

Our long-term goal is to create a physical SpiralCactus venue in Brussels: a flexible space envisioned for roughly 500 people, with professional sound and light, modular scenography, club nights, artist residencies, workshops, exhibitions and community events. We are currently developing this vision; SpiralCactus does not yet operate a permanent venue.

WHERE WE WANT TO GO

A living cultural home

We are not trying to become just another party promoter. SpiralCactus aims to connect underground scenes, offer emerging artists real opportunities, bring together sound, light and visual storytelling, and create a lasting cultural meeting point in Brussels. Our long-term ambition is to open a permanent physical venue where club nights coexist with daytime workshops, exhibitions, artist residencies, community projects and collaborations—an inclusive space that can grow with the city. This venue is a future project, not an existing location.
