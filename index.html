A place for the future

Today, SpiralCactus creates independent events across Brussels. Tomorrow, our ambition is to build a permanent physical home: an immersive, inclusive cultural venue with room for underground nights, sound, visual art, workshops, artist residencies and real connections. That venue is a long-term ambition and is not open yet.
